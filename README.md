### Ἦθος — About

Computer Science student at **McGill University** with a Minor in Statistics, based in Montreal.
I started with zero coding experience and a drive to build. I found my *why* while analyzing my dragon boat team's fitness data:
I don't just want to write code, I want to extract meaning from data and make everyday operations easier.

Today I build end-to-end data pipelines and operational analytics that solve real problems.
I'm currently a **Data Quality Intern at GoMaterials**, where I monitor pipelines and use LLM-driven processing to standardize supplier catalogs.

Beyond the screen: competitive dragon boat paddler for five years, president of **McGill Dragon Boat Z**, and an avid hiker.
